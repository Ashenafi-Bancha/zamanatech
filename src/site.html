FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Get in touch and we'll answer it directly.

What services does ZamanaTech offer?

We build websites, mobile apps for Android and iOS, ERP and business management systems, SaaS platforms, Telegram bots and mini apps. We also offer AI solutions, cloud and cybersecurity services, IT consulting, logo, branding and business card design, and ZamanaMenu, our digital menu and management platform for hospitality businesses.

What is ZamanaMenu?

ZamanaMenu is our digital menu and business management platform for modern hospitality. Hotels, cafés, restaurants and lounges replace printed menus with QR-code menus that guests open on their phones, update prices and items instantly, and manage their business from one place. Contact us for a demo.

Why does my business need a website?

A website works for you 24 hours a day. It helps customers find you on Google, shows them your products and services, builds trust in your brand and makes it easy for them to call, message, book or buy, even outside working hours.

I already have a Facebook or Telegram page. Do I still need a website?

Yes. Social media is great for reaching people, but you don't own it and it rarely shows up when customers search on Google. A website gives you a professional home you control. Use social media to attract people, and your website to turn them into customers.

Will a website really bring me more customers?

A well-built website makes you visible to people already searching for what you offer, answers their questions and gives them a clear way to contact you. Combined with your social media and a good Google presence, it becomes a steady source of new inquiries.

Is a website useful for schools, NGOs and other organizations?

Absolutely. Organizations use websites to share news and announcements, accept applications and registrations online, publish reports, show their impact to partners and donors, and communicate with the public in one trusted place.

Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build cross-platform mobile apps that run on both Android and iOS from one codebase, so you reach more customers with less cost and effort.

Do you work with small and local businesses?

Yes. Helping local businesses go digital is central to our mission. We work with shops, restaurants, schools, hotels and service companies to get them ready for the digital economy and Digital Ethiopia 2030.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A business website is usually much quicker than a full ERP or mobile platform. After our first conversation we give you a clear timeline with milestones, so you always know what comes next.

How much does a website or app cost?

Every project is different, so we price it after we understand your goals and the features you need. You receive a clear proposal with the scope and cost before any work begins.

Can you help us promote our business online?

Yes. Besides building your website or app, we help you build a strong presence on social media and online channels, with branding, design and digital tools that bring in more customers.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Launch is not the end. We monitor, maintain and improve your product, and we are here when you need new features or help.

Where are you located, and do you work remotely?

Our office is in Wolaita Sodo, Ethiopia. We work with clients across Ethiopia and internationally, meeting in person or online, whichever suits you.
